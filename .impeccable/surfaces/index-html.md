Mode: Persuade. Surface: index.html, a one-page personal brand site for María Daniela Guerrero that works as portfolio, brand presentation and client channel.

User steers:
- Direction round (structured fallback after the decision page closed): "una combinación; lo importante es mostrar que es modelo, debe ser elegante, pero le gustan sus raíces: lo de Nariño y lo de comparsa; unifica lo mejor de ambos mundos; usa animación three.js, transición, y que quede excelente en móvil".
- Iteration 2: type must be more elegant and clearer, with more life. The hero must be more interactive, with a Carnaval animation and something "marketera". Dani's own collage pins part of her taste: a cream ground, red script lettering, red halftone star and lips, dotted oval bubbles with "PROFESIONAL EN / Diseño de modas" and "pero marketera / DE CORAZÓN", and an oval portrait. The site must read as super professional and full of life.
- Iteration 3: Formación, the UGC section and "Lo que hago" must improve. UGC should show the brand logos so visitors see she has created content with big brands. Formación needs more style. The closing section should be more interactive and cooler. Communication strategy, campaigns and advertising should show more. Her Canva reference: "¿Qué tanto hago?" with photo tiles per service, halftone headphones and a "barbie marketera" bubble.

THESIS: The page is one colcha de retazos sewn from Negros to Blancos. Fashion-editorial elegance (she is a model and a fashion designer) sits on the Carnaval's own black and talco. The saturated retazos of the Nariño patchwork fields and her comparsa vest are stitched in as color, and her own collage lettering (red script, halftone stickers) is her voice on top. The textile is the unifier: the colcha of the Andean fields, the comparsa patchwork and a fashion designer's fabric are one material. It refuses the influencer canon (white page, serif name, full-bleed portrait, grey logo row) and the fiesta collage of a generic Carnaval site.

OWN-WORLD:
- Grounds: Negro de Día de Negros #0D0C0E and talco #F3F4F6, the cool white of Carnaval powder. Crema #E9E4D1 is the paper of Dani's own collage, used for Sobre mí and as sticker paper (#EDE7D3). The user pinned it through her artwork.
- Retazo colors, used as whole section fabrics and as patches: lila papa en flor #BBA7E6, cebada #CFDD4C, verde papa #1F4A30, tierra arada #6B321E, trigo #E3A93A. Cobalto #2D4BD8 and naranja #F36B21 appear only inside patchwork and confeti.
- Carmín from her collage: CTA #B3121F (hover #8F0E19, 6.3:1 with talco text); script ink #C8102E on light grounds and #F23A44 on dark grounds (both 4.5:1 or better); halftone print #E5242F.
- Component language:
  - Every section is a retazo of a different fabric, top-stitched to the next with a dashed seam.
  - Services form a bento of six photo tiles (her own production BTS: content grid, gimbal, shoot, ARTIS packaging, desk, BEE). The strategy tile is the largest. Each tile carries a stitched inner frame and always shows its ficha (what it includes, and "Ideal para"). There is no toggle: on desktop the text sits over a deep scrim on the photo, and on phones the photo sits above the text. A halftone headphones sticker with a "barbie marketera" bubble sits beside the title "¿Qué tanto hago por tu marca?".
  - Client logos are the quilt: their own square tiles stitched onto green fabric, portrait logos contained, plus one carmín patch inviting the next brand.
  - Bullets are stitched retazo squares; a mini-colcha nav shows the page as its patchwork.
  - Collage stickers: halftone star and lips on die-cut paper, and dotted oval bubbles.
  - A carmín cinta (comparsa ribbon, pageant sash) carries the services.
  - The UGC wall: the brands' own marks in negro on stitched cebada tiles that invert to negro on hover and link to each sample video, plus a carmín tile inviting the next product. Copy frames them as samples ("Videos UGC de muestra que he creado con productos de estas marcas", each tag "Reel de muestra" or "TikTok de muestra") and never says the brands hired her; a tile may drop "de muestra" only if Dani confirms that video was a paid brief.
  - Formación: garment hang tags (lila, cebada, papel, talco) with a grommet, stitched edge and string, hanging from a papel rail; a tap or hover sets them swinging. The kind of credential (Profesional, Maestría, Certificación) sits in the foot line with the institution, never as a kicker above the title.
  - Contacto: "Arma tu mensaje", a papel card with chip choices (what the brand needs, what kind of brand). It composes the WhatsApp message live into a real wa.me link (no script-opened window) and throws confeti from the button. The confeti instance is created on the first choice and disposed when the section leaves view.
- Type: Bodoni Moda for display, with optical size lowered at mid sizes for clarity. Yellowtail script in carmín is reserved for Dani's own phrases. Figtree for text, and tracked caps only inside sticker lockups and labels.
- Navigation: a sticky negro header that steps aside while scrolling down and returns on scroll up, with the current section stitched under its link. On phones the menu is a full sheet of Bodoni links with the WhatsApp action and socials. The footer repeats wordmark, sections, action and a way back up.
- Photography is the content: white-studio editorial portraits for the model, Carnaval comparsa and Srta. Nariño Bambuco 2025 for the roots.

STORY: A business owner or brand lead arrives from Instagram on a phone. They see Dani as a model first and read in one line that she is a Content & Marketing Strategist who plans, creates and stars in content. They believe it through real proof:
- five services with clear fichas;
- a four-step "¿Cómo empezamos?" path;
- 17 client brands and four credentials;
- Srta. Nariño Bambuco 2025, and dancer and artist of the Carnaval de Negros y Blancos;
- UGC samples, always framed as samples.

They tap "Escríbeme y lo charlamos", or build their message at the close, and land in WhatsApp with a ready-made brief. Section order: hero (negro), sobre mí (crema), escena (negro), servicios (talco), marcas (verde papa), UGC (cebada), formación (tierra), contacto (talco).

Signature interaction and motion grammar ("retazos y talco"):
- The portrait breaks into an irregular colcha of stitched fabric patches that flip in a diagonal sweep (exponential ease-out) into the next look. Tap the portrait for the next look; double-tap it for a like, a carmín heart that bursts into confeti (the marketera gesture).
- The hero air holds talco and confeti from the Carnaval de Blancos. The pointer is wind, and a tap on the hero throws a handful of talco.
- Stickers bob gently and can be dragged anywhere; releasing one throws a little talco.
- Dani's script phrases write themselves in as they enter view, and the cinta scrolls slowly.
- One pause control stops all hero motion, and hover pauses the cinta.
- Outside the hero: service photos zoom on hover, hang tags swing, UGC tiles invert, and the closing send throws confeti.
- Reduced motion: no particles, no autoplay, no bob or marquee, and script is always visible. Without WebGL the static image and swatches swap looks. Save-Data skips the three.js bundle.
